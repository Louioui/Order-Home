## Bulgogi image generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape food photograph for a native iPhone dinner-planning app recipe card. Primary request: a realistic, appetizing Korean bulgogi rice bowl photographed from overhead. Subject: thin, curled slices of caramelized soy-marinated beef over fluffy white jasmine rice in a simple cream ceramic bowl, garnished with finely sliced scallions and sesame seeds, with neat portions of thin carrot ribbons and crisp cucumber slices. Scene/backdrop: a warm neutral kitchen tabletop, uncluttered. Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape image; one generously filled bowl centered with enough rim visible to crop cleanly into portrait and square recipe cards; food is the clear hero. Lighting/mood: warm natural window light, inviting editorial food photography, soft shadows, lifelike textures and restrained colors. Constraints: beef must be thinly sliced Korean bulgogi, visibly rice underneath; no large steak, no chips or fries, no cutlery crossing the bowl, no people, no text, no logo, no watermark.
