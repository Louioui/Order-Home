Stop the Group Chat Chaos

Asking "What do you want to eat?" into a silent family group chat invites dread. Order Home replaces unstructured texting with a structured, 5-minute dinner ballot. Nominate up to five cravings and cast votes in seconds.

The "Table for Two" Compromise Blocker

Couples often end up with mediocre takeout because one person compromised on a dish they secretly disliked. In Couple Mode, Order Home introduces strict consensus matching: a recipe only locks when both partners explicitly vote "Love".

  • Strict mutual-love validation stops resentment dinners cold.
  • Zero compromise rule: If neither loves a choice, the ballot resets instantly.
  • Dynamic attendance toggle: Skip or eat out without skewing portions.
  • Personalized House Menu favorites built over time.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Order Home require an account, email login, or subscription?
No. Order Home is built on a 100% offline-first architecture. It functions completely out of the box with zero logins, zero accounts, zero subscriptions, and zero advertising trackers. Your dinner data belongs to you.
How does hands-free voice control work when my hands are messy?
Inside the Cooking Studio, tap the microphone badge to enable Hands-Free mode. Apple's on-device speech recognition listens for simple kitchen keywords like "Next", "Back", or "Ring Bell". No audio is ever recorded, transmitted, or saved to any cloud server.
How does the couple compromise blocker prevent arguments?
In Couple Mode, Order Home requires explicit mutual love ("Love" reaction from both partners) for a winning dish to lock. If one person votes "Down" or "Pass", the meal is rejected and cannot win. This eliminates passive-aggressive dinners and guarantees both people actually want what's on the table.
Can I import custom YouTube or creator recipes?
Yes! Tap the "+" button in the Menu Builder or House Menu and paste any valid YouTube or recipe URL. Order Home sanitizes the link, extracts creator attribution, and embeds distraction-free playback directly inside your step-by-step kitchen ticket.
How do I permanently delete all my household data?
In compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), Order Home includes a dedicated, destructive "Reset Kitchen & Delete All Data" option located directly inside the Household settings tab. Tapping this permanently purges local sandbox databases, shared App Group containers, and active Live Activities in one instant action.
Legal & Privacy Disclosure

Order Home Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Complies with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1
Our Privacy Philosophy: What you eat at home with your family is nobody's business. Order Home was architected from line one to collect zero personal data, zero analytics, zero location records, and zero advertising IDs.

1. Complete Offline-First Architecture

Order Home operates as a native, local-first iOS application. All household configurations, member display names, dinner ratings, voting tallies, recipes, and rolling 60-dinner history records are stored exclusively in your device's local application sandbox and private iOS App Group container (group.com.partnershipwithmedia.orderhome).

2. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Zero Advertising

We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash trackers, or behavior profiling networks. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose any household dining records to data brokers or third parties under any circumstances.

3. On-Device Speech Recognition & Apple Intelligence

When you enable hands-free kitchen voice navigation, audio analysis is performed strictly on your Apple device using Apple's Speech and AVAudioEngine frameworks. Order Home never records, transmits, or uploads voice samples or ambient kitchen conversations to any external server. Siri and Apple Intelligence App Intents run strictly through Apple's native on-device intent dispatcher.

4. Apple Reminders & System Permissions

Order Home requests access to your Reminders app solely when you tap "Add to Apple Reminders" on a locked Kitchen Ticket. Access is used strictly to create and populate a dedicated list named "Order Home: Tonight's Dinner" with missing groceries. Order Home never inspects, modifies, or accesses any of your other private Reminders calendars or personal tasks.

5. Optional Cloud Kitchen Sync

If your household chooses to configure Cloud Kitchen sync, dinner states synchronize directly to your private, self-managed database via encrypted HTTPS connections. Authentication tokens and database secrets remain encrypted in the iOS Keychain. You may disconnect or revert to local-only mode at any time with zero data lock-in.

6. In-App Account & Complete Data Erasure (Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You have full, autonomous control over your data. In full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), Order Home provides an unambiguous in-app data deletion mechanism:

  • Local Kitchen Wipe: Navigate to the Household tab and tap "Reset Kitchen & Delete All Data". Confirming this action immediately unlinks and permanently erases all recipes, voting histories, dinner ratings, household profiles, App Group container caches, and active Live Activities.
  • Cloud Kitchen Disconnect: If connected to a shared kitchen, tapping "Delete Account & All Data" signs your device out, revokes authorization keys, and purges all shared cache files from your device.

7. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your dining data, please contact our privacy officer at privacy@orderhome.app or via mail at Order Home Software, Support Division.
